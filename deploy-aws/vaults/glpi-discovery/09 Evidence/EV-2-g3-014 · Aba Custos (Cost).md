# EV-2-g3-014 · Aba Custos (Cost)

> [!quote] source/tabs/cost.rst — "Cost"
> "Costs can be linked to the budget in order to estimate whether an expense will fit within the allocated budget."
> Tipos de custo: **Time cost**, **Fixed cost**, **Material cost**.
> **Add a cost**: **Add a new cost** e preencher a informação. "If you enter a budget, this expense will be deducted from the budget in question."
> "You can modify a cost later once it has been validated."
> **Delete a cost**: clicar no custo (info aparece no topo), **Delete permanently**. Se um orçamento foi informado, a despesa é removida dele. "Once deleted, you will not be able to go back, you will have to enter the cost manually again."
