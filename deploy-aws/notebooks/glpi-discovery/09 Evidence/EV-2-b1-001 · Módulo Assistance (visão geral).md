# EV-2-b1-001 · Módulo Assistance (visão geral)

> [!quote] source/modules/assistance/index.rst — "Assistance"
> "Assistance module allows users to create, follow and process tickets. Plannings, statistics and recurrent tickets are also available."
> "Depending on profile permissions, it is possible as well to create, follow and process problems and changes."
> "GLPI assistance module is compliant with ITIL best practises guide and integrates therefore some fields normalization. However, following ITIL best practises is recommended but not mandatory and it is perfectly feasible, thanks to module's flexibility, to implement an assistance service tailored to the organization's needs."
> Submódulos (toctree): actors, prioritymatrix, tickets, problems, changes, lifecyclematrix, planning, statistics, categories.
> "Tickets, Changes and Problems can be viewed globally in a Kanban view."

Pontos-chave:
- O módulo permite **criar, acompanhar e processar** tickets; oferece também planejamentos, estatísticas e tickets recorrentes.
- Acesso a **problemas e mudanças** depende das permissões do perfil.
- Aderência ao ITIL é **recomendada, não obrigatória**; há flexibilidade para adaptar o serviço à organização.
- Tickets, mudanças e problemas têm visão **Kanban** global.
