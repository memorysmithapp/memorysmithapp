# EV-2-c2-005 · Racks

> [!quote] modules/assets/racks.rst · "Racks"
> "A rack is a piece of equipment such as a patch bay or computer cabinet used to organize, host and physically connect different network equipments and servers in a datacenter or technical room. A rack is a dedicated object in GLPI that virtually represents the physical structure of a rack or cabinet."
> Objetivos: **Organize equipment** (visualizar/gerir servidores, switches, routers, UPS...), **Facilitate physical management** (rastrear onde o equipamento está instalado), **Optimize space** (uso eficiente do espaço, deixar espaço para novos equipamentos).

> [!quote] Campos ao adicionar (+ Add)
> Name, Data center position, Location, Technician in charge, Group in charge, Serial number, User, Comments, Position in room, Number of units, Height, Max. Power (in watts), Max. weight, Status, Rack type, Manufacturer, Model, Inventory number, Group, Server room, Door orientation in room (North, East, South, West), Width, Depth, Measured power (in watts), Background color.

> [!quote] Abas
> Items ("The Item tab shows the position of each element in the rack. You can modify, add or remove an item from your park to your rack"); Impact Analysis; Contracts; Documents; Tickets; Problems; Changes; Reservations; Historical; All.
