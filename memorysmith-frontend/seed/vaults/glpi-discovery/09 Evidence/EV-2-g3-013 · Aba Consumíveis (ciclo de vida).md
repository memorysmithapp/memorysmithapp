# EV-2-g3-013 · Aba Consumíveis (ciclo de vida)

> [!quote] source/tabs/consumables.rst — "Consumables"
> "The first table lists the consumables that are new. The second table lists the consumables identified as used."
> **Add in new status**: ao criar, selecionar o número necessário e **Add consumables** (um ou mais); status padrão = **new**.
> **Change in used status**: marcar o consumível (check box), **Actions**, **Give**, selecionar grupos ou usuários, e a entrada desejada. "when the consumable is used, you can change the owner ... by repeating the operation".
> **Back to stock**: marcar, **Actions**, **Back to stock**, **Post**.
> **Delete**: marcar, **Actions**, **Delete permanently**, **Post**. "You can delete a consumable whether it is new or used".
