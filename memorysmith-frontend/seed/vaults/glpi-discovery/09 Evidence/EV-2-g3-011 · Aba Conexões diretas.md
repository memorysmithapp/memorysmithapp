# EV-2-g3-011 · Aba Conexões diretas

> [!quote] source/tabs/connections.rst — "Connections"
> "This field lists all the devices that have either been detected by the automatic inventory or added manually."
> Materiais reagrupados: **Device, Monitor, Phone, Printers**.
> **Add a connection**: selecionar o **type of the device**, selecionar o **materiel**, clicar em **Connect**. Se o equipamento não está na lista, deve ser adicionado direto ao ativo (device, monitor, phone, printers).
> **Delete a connection**: feita a partir da entrada de cada hardware conectado via **mass actions** (marcar check box, botão **Actions**).
> "Every deletion or addition of a connection is recorded in the history of the computer."
