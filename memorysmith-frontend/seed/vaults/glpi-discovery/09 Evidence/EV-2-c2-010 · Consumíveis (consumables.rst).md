# EV-2-c2-010 · Consumíveis

> [!quote] modules/assets/consumables.rst · "Consumables"
> "Consumables are items relating to resources or supplies intended to be used, loaned or entrusted as part of maintenance operations or the day-to-day running of an IT estate. These consumables generally include items such as USB sticks, HDMI cables or spare parts for hardware."
> Nota: "Consumables cannot be managed by the agent or automatic inventory."
> Ações específicas: "Add new consumables to a model"; listar consumíveis alocados via botão de menu **Summary** ("allows you to see a summary of allocated consumables").

> [!quote] Campos do formulário (modelo de consumível)
> Name, Consumable type, Technician in charge, Group in charge, User, Comments, Picture, Location, Reference, Manufacturer, Inventory number, Group, Alert threshold, Stock target.
