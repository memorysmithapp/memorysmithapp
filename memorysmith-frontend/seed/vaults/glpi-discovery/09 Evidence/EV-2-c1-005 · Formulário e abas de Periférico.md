# EV-2-c1-005 · Formulário e abas de Periférico

> [!quote] modules/assets/peripherals.rst
> "Peripherals object refers to ancillary equipment or accessories connected to a main system (computer, server, etc.). This includes hardware elements required to operate or extend the capabilities of a workstation or network."

**Campos do formulário de Periférico:** Name; Location; Technician in charge; Group in charge; Alternate username number; Alternate username; User; Group; Comments; Status; **Device type**; Manufacturer; Model; Serial number; Inventory number; **Management type**; Network; UUID; Update source.

> [!note] Templates: "It is possible to use templates with peripherals."

**Abas:** Impact Analysis; Operating systems; Software; Components (BIOS, Processor, Memory, Hard Drive, Network card, Drive, Battery, Graphics card, Soundcard, Controller); Lines; Connections (Device, Monitor, Phone, Printers); Network Ports; Sockets; Management; Contracts; Documents; Knowledge Base; Tickets; Problems; Changes; Projects; Links; Locks; Notes; Reservations; Certificates; Domains; Appliances; Import information; Historical; All.

**Ações:** periféricos não têm ações específicas — usar as ações comuns.
